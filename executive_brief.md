# Executive Brief: Socratic Voice Companion for Calistung Comprehension

> **Status:** Validation-Ready PRD (Risk Tier 2)  
> **Decision Requested:** Authorize Phase 1 engineering build and closed beta field deployment.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

We propose deploying an affordable, voice-first Socratic companion mobile application to build foundational Calistung (reading, writing, arithmetic) comprehension in preschool children [EV-03]. The product replaces passive screen entertainment with structured 15-minute daily voice inquiry sessions that build verifiable cognitive reasoning [EV-06, EV-13]. With a hybrid cloud architecture and static prompt caching, the initiative guarantees a minimum 70% gross profit margin by strictly capping monthly inference costs at IDR 25,000 per child [EV-04, EV-05].

---

## 2. Problem & Cost of Inaction

Middle-class parents face an unacceptable trade-off between expensive private tutors (IDR 500,000 to IDR 2,000,000/month) and hollow gamified mobile apps that hook children on dopamine loops without teaching foundational skills [EV-12, EV-14]. National PISA scores demonstrate severe deficiencies in foundational literacy and numeracy across emerging markets [EV-09]. Doing nothing forfeits the early childhood cognitive development segment to passive video platforms and permanently locks our brand out of multi-year learner lifetime value [EV-19, EV-20].

---

## 3. Desired Outcomes

- **Gross Margin Target:** Maintain gross margins equal to or exceeding 70% across all cohorts [EV-04].
- **COGS Cap:** Keep operational cloud inference costs below IDR 25,000 ($1.55) per monthly active child [EV-05].
- **Pedagogical Mastery:** Lift child Unassisted Response Rate (URR) by 25% within 60 days of usage [EV-36].
- **System SLA:** Maintain P95 conversational turn latency under 1,000ms on 4G cellular links [EV-31].

---

## 4. Proposed Solution & Behavior Shift

The companion engages children aged 3 to 6 in daily 15-minute spoken dialogues [EV-06, EV-21]. When a child struggles, the system executes a Socratic step-down ladder, asking simpler inducing questions rather than stating the answer [EV-34]. If ambient room noise degrades voice capture, large visual cards appear dynamically to maintain engagement [EV-35]. 

This transitions family habits from passive, high-guilt video scrolling to independent verbal exploration. Sessions conclude automatically with a calm closing ritual to prevent screen obsession [EV-07].
